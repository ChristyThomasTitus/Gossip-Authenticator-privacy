🫖 The Gossip Authenticator

Privacy Policy · Last updated 3 November 2026

The Gossip Authenticator is a free app with no ads, made for fun. This page explains what the app stores, who can see it, and how to delete it. We tried to write it so a human can read it.

What we collect

DataWhy
Email address and passwordTo create your account and sign you in. The password is stored hashed; we never see it.
Username and display nameSo your friends know who posted what.
Circles you create or join, and invite codesTo show you the right feeds.
Gossip, details, and reactions you postThat's the whole app.
Reports and blocks you makeTo keep circles safe and to hide people you block.

We do not collect your contacts, location, photos, device identifiers, or advertising IDs. There are no ads and no analytics trackers in the app.

Who can see what you post

Everything you post is visible to the members of that circle and nobody else. Circles are private and joined only by invite code. People you block can't be seen by you; your posts remain visible to others in the circle.

Please remember the app is about gossip. Don't post private information about people who aren't in on the joke. Content that gets reported by three members is hidden automatically and reviewed.

Where it's stored

Data is stored with Supabase, a hosting provider, in the region chosen when the project was created. It's protected by row-level security rules, which means the database itself only serves you rows you're allowed to see.

Sharing and selling

We don't sell, rent, or share your data with anyone. The only third party that touches it is the hosting provider above. We will disclose data only if the law requires it.

Deleting your data

Open the app → ⚙️ Settings → Delete my account. This immediately and permanently removes your profile, every gossip and detail you posted, your reactions, reports and blocks. Circles you created stay for the other members.

You can also email us (below) from the address on your account and we'll delete it for you.

Children

The app is not for anyone under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We don't knowingly keep data from children; tell us and we'll delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes and the new version appears here.
